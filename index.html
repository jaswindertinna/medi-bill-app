Profit (incl. GST) = full sale value minus full purchase cost, with GST included in both — your real margin. Profit (simple) = sale rate − purchase rate, with no GST adjustment at all — a quick per-unit check. "After expenses" deducts shop expenses recorded for the same period on the Expenses tab. Needs a purchase rate recorded for each medicine to be accurate.
